## The third seat strengthens the rule

A failed probe from one seat cannot adjudicate a vantage-local, held-safe, input-driven surface on
another seat. The correct evidence topology is:

```text
origin seat       -> local fabric/receipt/process observation
attack-verify seat-> independent route or artifact check
third seat        -> public-code/test/receipt reproduction
CI                -> immutable-head execution where applicable
```

A third seat that also cannot reach a loopback route does not create two votes that make the route
false. It creates two `CANNOT_SEE` observations. The owning seat's live receipt remains valid within
its timestamped scope.

## Probe taxonomy

Every unsuccessful observation should be classified before “broken” is considered:

```text
CANNOT_SEE            vantage lacks route/device/body
ROUTE_BOUNDARY         endpoint exists under another path or policy
HELD_SAFE              waiting for agent/operator input by design
STALE                   artifact exists but is not current liveness proof
FALLBACK                degraded sensor, not the owning live surface
UNVERIFIED_CURRENT      historical success exists; present reachability not reconfirmed
ACTUAL_FAILURE          owning evidence shows the intended function failed
```

Only the final class supports a failure claim.

## No-inflate companion

`freeze != broken` does not mean every frozen descriptor is live. A held queue proves pending state,
not completed execution. An HBP packet proves preserved intent/evidence, not physical fire. A
historical heartbeat proves historical liveness, not permanent current health.

## Evidence retention

When pixels, dashboards, or routes are unavailable, preserve:

```text
route attempted
timestamp and vantage
HBP/HBI row or source pointer
commit/source hash
fallback/stale markers
live_ack=0
claim_live_success=0
```

This lets another seat or a later run resolve the boundary without erasing the underlying packet.

## Authority

Independent verification may reclassify a false-down observation. It cannot veto, approve, dispatch,
restart, mint, or fire the held-safe lane unless the documented operator/cosign authority separately
permits it.

## Merge rule

Merge additive receipts, route corrections, tests, and honest demotions when clean. Hold only real
failures, conflicts, private material, authority changes, or unsupported current-liveness upgrades.
